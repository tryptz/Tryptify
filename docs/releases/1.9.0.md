# Tryptify 1.9.0

This release is mostly about your account: what you change on your phone now reaches your account, and your account no longer quietly undoes it. Downloads also come out properly tagged, with their cover inside.

## Changed

- **Deleted things stay deleted.** Unliking a track, deleting a playlist or taking a song out of one could undo itself on the next launch, because the delete never reached your account. Each change is now held until your account confirms it.
- **Changes made offline catch up.** Favourites, playlists, and EQ and mixer presets edited without a connection are sent once you are back online. Before, favourites and playlists only went up when you pressed Sync, and presets got one try.
- **Devices no longer wipe each other's settings.** Saving settings from one device used to erase every setting it did not have itself. Each device now only adds or changes what it touched, and a setting changed offline is not undone at the next launch.
- **Signing in keeps trying to restore your settings.** If fetching your settings fails, the app retries instead of carrying on with the device's own, which could then overwrite the copy saved to your account.
- **Waiting changes stay with their account.** A change that has not gone through yet is only ever sent to the account it was made under. Switching accounts before it does will not hand it to the other one.
- **Downloaded FLACs carry their own tags.** Title, album, track and disc number, album artist, year and genre are written into every downloaded FLAC, so players like Auxio or Symfonium file it under the right album, in order.
- **Cover art is inside the file.** Each downloaded track has its album cover embedded, as well as saved beside it. TIDAL downloads were getting no cover at all and get one now.

## Known limitation

- **A delete on one phone can still come back from another.** If a second device presses Sync before it has picked up the delete, it uploads its own copy again. Fixing that needs a change on the server side.

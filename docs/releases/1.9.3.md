# Tryptify 1.9.3

This release is mostly about glass and TIDAL: the mini player, the nav bar and the player's controls now bend what is behind them like real glass, and TIDAL works properly again, with playlists that open, search that finds artists and albums, and songs that play instead of skipping. Downloads are filed by artist and album, TIDAL's Dolby Atmos mixes can be downloaded, and each service streams and downloads in a quality of its own. A USB DAC in exclusive mode finally has a volume of its own, and it starts quiet. And the lyrics get god rays: light that streams from the line being sung and runs on under the player's glass, on letters that can be made of any glass you like.

Each change is marked New, Changed, Removed or Fixed.

## Look and feel

- New: **Liquid glass.** The mini player, the nav bar, search bars and panels bend whatever is behind them at their rounded edges, the way real glass does, and stay clear across the middle.
- New: **New glass themes.** Clear, Tinted and Tilt, after iOS, then Pure, Droplet, Prism, Bubble, Mercury, Ice, Halo, Aurora, Dusk and Holo, in Player Visuals Studio. Clear is the new starting look; glass you tuned yourself is kept.
- New: **Shadows under the bars.** The nav bar and the mini player cast the same drop shadow as the play button, from the shadow depth, softness and tint in Player Visuals Studio.
- Changed: **One clean glass edge.** Glass catches the light as one rim at its edge, instead of a second bright line inside it that made a pane look like two layers.
- Changed: **The mini player matches the nav bar.** Its glass takes the nav bar's colour rather than the album's, so the two read as one piece of glass. Its text and cover still follow the album.
- Changed: **Song titles get the whole line.** Every song list is laid out like Library › Local: the title has its line to itself, and the artist, album, source and quality sit under it. Like a song from its menu or from the player.

## Now playing

- New: **A glass play button and dock.** The play button and the action dock bend the album art behind them too.
- New: **God rays on the lyrics.** Light streams out of the line being sung, or from a light behind the lyrics that the letters block, so their shadows streak through it. The shafts run on down the screen under the title, the controls and the dock, whose glass frosts and bends them. In Player Visuals Studio › Lyrics you drag the light around a dome to aim it, put it under the letters or over them, have it follow the sung word in word-timed lyrics, and add dust, a flare on the beat, an orbit, a sway or phone tilt. Off until you turn it on.
- New: **Lyrics in any glass.** The lyric letters take the same glass settings as the player: roundness, depth, surface motion, reflection, gloss, tilt, light angle, edge width and frost, and Match player glass copies the player's onto them. With god rays on, the letters catch their light, with glints that slide across them as they tilt.
- New: **Nine new lyric looks.** Sunburst, Cathedral, Eclipse, Daybreak, Searchlight, Spotlight, Crepuscular (the Shadertoy "Crepuscular light", as written), Mercury and Sea Glass. Your own look and the presets you had stay as they were.
- Changed: **A soft shadow under the lyrics.** The lyrics' shadow falls on the background under each line, soft and away from the light, instead of a dark copy stuck to every letter. Shadow depth sets how dark and how soft it is.
- Changed: **The Studio preview beats with the song.** Player Visuals Studio's preview of the song playing pulses with the song itself, as the player does, and is still while it is paused. The sample line, with no song behind it, keeps its steady beat.
- Fixed: **The legacy player fills the screen.** With Legacy player on in Settings › System › Performance, the controls sit at the bottom of the screen and the album art is as big as it fits, instead of a small cover over a band of empty space. Lyrics get the full width instead of a narrow square.

## TIDAL

- New: **The Dolby Atmos badge.** TIDAL songs and albums with a Dolby Atmos mix carry the badge in search, lists, album pages and on the player while that mix plays. The TIDAL Dolby Atmos switch is now in Settings › Audio, under TIDAL's quality, as well as under the spatial renderer.
- Fixed: **TIDAL playlists open.** A TIDAL playlist opened from search showed nothing. It loads now, hundreds of songs at a time, ready to play, though it can't be renamed or edited here.
- Fixed: **TIDAL search finds more than songs.** Artists, albums and playlists show up in TIDAL search, artist pages have their name, picture and similar artists, and TIDAL's own lyrics and radio work.
- Fixed: **TIDAL songs play instead of skipping.** On a server signed in with your own TIDAL account, every song skipped to the next one. They play now.

## Playback and quality

- New: **Play alongside other apps.** Turn it on in Settings › Audio and your music keeps playing under a game or a video, instead of pausing whenever another app makes a sound.
- Changed: **Quality per service.** TIDAL, Qobuz and Deezer each stream and download in a quality of their own, named in their own terms: AAC or MP3, CD or Hi-Res FLAC. A Streaming | Download switch in Settings › Audio picks which one you are setting.
- Fixed: **Skipping songs no longer crashes the app.** Skipping from one song to the next, most often in a Dolby Atmos or surround playlist, could close the app.
- Fixed: **16-bit FLAC plays cleanly on hi-res output.** On some phones a 16-bit FLAC on the hi-res output played as loud static at double speed. It plays as it should now.

## Equalizer

- New: **AutoEQ per device.** Like Poweramp's per-output presets: the devices button on an AutoEQ preset assigns it to the phone speaker, wired headphones, Bluetooth or USB, or to one Bluetooth headphone or USB DAC by name, which comes before its kind. When that output connects, its preset switches in and a short message says so, with the app in the background too. **AutoEQ off** can be assigned like a preset, so the speaker can play without the correction meant for your headphones. Outputs nobody assigned leave the EQ as it is.

## Exclusive USB DAC

- New: **A volume for USB DACs.** With a DAC in exclusive mode, the player has a glass volume bar under the play controls, and the volume keys bring up the same bar at the top of any screen. With the app in the background or the screen locked, the keys still reach the DAC and Android's own volume panel shows its level. Muting remembers the level, and unmuting goes back to it.
- Changed: **USB DACs start quiet.** Every time a DAC is claimed it starts at −24 dB and fades in over half a second, instead of playing at full volume. The level moves in even 2 dB steps from silence to full, and smoothly, without clicks.
- Fixed: **Volume works at every bit depth.** 24-bit and 32-bit output played at full volume whatever the volume was set to. It follows the volume now, and at full volume the audio still reaches the DAC bit-perfect.
- Fixed: **DAC volume stays with the DAC.** The volume keys on a DAC used to turn down the app's own volume, which then left the speaker and Bluetooth quiet with no way to turn it back up. That volume is set back to full once, on this update.

## Downloads

- New: **Artist and album folders.** Downloads to a folder you picked are filed as Artist / Album / 01. Title, with one cover.jpg per album and tags other players can group by.
- New: **Dolby Atmos downloads.** TIDAL's download quality can be Dolby Atmos: a song with an Atmos mix downloads it untouched, as an .m4a, and the rest as Hi-Res FLAC.
- Changed: **Downloads opens at once.** It said there were no downloads for a few seconds before showing them. Songs you put in the download folder yourself now show their own titles, artists and covers.
- Fixed: **Hi-Res downloads fall back to CD.** When TIDAL sends a Hi-Res song in pieces your server can't join into one file, it downloads as CD FLAC instead of failing.
- Fixed: **No broken files left behind.** A download that fails partway no longer leaves half a file in your folder, and names starting with a dot (.38 Special) no longer hide songs from other players.

## Library

- New: **Better folder browsing.** Sort folders by name, number of songs or newest file, and songs by original order, file name, title, artist, album, year, duration, date modified, file type, quality or file size. The choice applies to every folder and is remembered. Tap any part of the path above a folder to jump straight up to it, and Play or Shuffle a folder together with every folder inside it, from its page or by long-pressing it.
- New: **Titles from file names.** Settings › Library can name local songs after their files instead of their title tags, for files whose tags are wrong or shared. Renaming a file then renames the song.
- Fixed: **Singles stay with their album.** A song downloaded on its own, next to the album it is from, no longer shows up as a second copy of that album. Rescan the library to regroup it.
- Fixed: **Folders play in their real order.** Songs in a folder go in album, disc and track order, and files without track numbers in file-name order ("2" before "10"), instead of alphabetically by title, so an untagged live recording plays as it was performed.
- Fixed: **Rescans run to the end.** Rescan Library keeps going after you leave Settings, instead of stopping halfway.

## The app

- New: **Crash reports you can switch off.** A crash or freeze, even one deep in the audio engine, is saved to Downloads as a .log file to attach to a bug report. Settings › System › Diagnostics turns that off.
- Fixed: **Album and artist pages stop crashing.** Opening an album or an artist, especially one opened before, could close the app.

# Tryptify 1.9.4

Your own music as a galaxy, stats and achievements, Spotify playlists from a link, and exclusive mode on more USB DACs, Apple's USB-C adapter among them.

Each change is marked New, Changed, Removed or Fixed.

- New: **Your library as a galaxy.** Under Folders in Library: every artist on your phone is a star beside their genre, their albums are planets and their tracks moons. Tap a moon to play it; long-press a star to list everything. Works offline.
- Changed: **Short tracks.** Intros, interludes and skits under 30 seconds now come in with their albums. Voice notes and app sounds stay out unless they're in a folder you picked.
- New: **Song row badges.** Settings › Appearance › Song row badges turns each mark on a song row (source, quality, format, download, length) on or off.
- New: **Spotify playlists from a link.** Paste a playlist link in Create playlist to import it. Public playlists bring their first 100 songs; for more, a private playlist or Liked Songs, open Exportify inside the app and sign in with any Spotify account.
- New: **Track info.** In a song's menu: its format, rate, depth, bitrate, size and every tag, ready to copy. The menu itself is glass now.
- New: **Search and sort Downloads**, like Liked Songs, plus sorting by file size.
- Fixed: **Recently played keeps your local songs.** Every song is recorded once it starts, whatever it plays from. Pull down on Library to refresh.
- Fixed: **Track info for MP4 files** shows the real format and channels, so 5.1 AAC and ALAC read correctly.

## Stats and your account

- New: **Stats to dig into.** Time listened, when you listen, top artist, what's on repeat, new artists, your streak and audio quality, and every card opens a chart.
- New: **Achievements.** Ten goals, from a 30-day streak to trying the mixer and World radio, each with your progress and a button that takes you there.
- New: **Your picture and username.** The top bar's account button shows your picture or initials. Pick your own photo and username on the Account page.

## Discover

- New: **Search the galaxy** for a genre, by any of its names, or an artist.
- New: **One sky, two galaxies.** Switch between your library and the genre galaxy from the title bar.
- Changed: **Galaxy and Timeline are two glass pills**, and Surprise me is in the title bar.
- Changed: **A stronger zoom** that stays on the planet you're zooming toward.

## Now playing

- Changed: **Long titles slide** to show the rest instead of ending in "…".
- Changed: **Glass buttons** for Visualizer and Waveform, a glass speed or BPM pill, and a glass pill that picks the speed unit.
- Fixed: **The visualizer no longer flashes** with vsync off.
- Fixed: **Glass corners** on the audio tools sheet run all the way round.
- Fixed: **BPM at the right tempo**, not half or double, up to 250. A song's BPM tag shows at once.
- Fixed: **Panels read in light themes.**

## Playback

- Fixed: **Seeking in Apple's ALAC files** on your phone. Apple Music streams start at once and play through, as before.
- Changed: **Blends need gapless.** With gapless off, the blend slider is greyed out and each song plays on its own.
- Changed: **No blends in exclusive mode.** With Exclusive USB DAC on, songs play one at a time, gapless; your blend comes back when it's off.

## Lyrics

- Fixed: **Songs named by number** ("2-05 Song") are found, and no longer get "01" as their artist. Refresh the library once.
- Fixed: **Compilations** are searched under each song's own artist.
- Fixed: **The right lyrics for your own files.**

## Exclusive USB DAC

- Changed: **More DACs**, including Apple's USB-C adapter, at 48 kHz and 24-bit.
- Changed: **DACs at their full output.** The DAC's own volume is set to 0 dB, and the app's volume is the only one; it starts at −24 dB.
- Fixed: **DACs that refused every song** play now. A rate the DAC can't take plays through Android instead.
- Changed: **One volume bar**, at the top of every screen, from the volume keys.
- Fixed: **No full-volume burst between songs.** Every sample is limited before the volume, leaving room for EQ boosts of up to 12 dB.
- Changed: **The DAC card shows the DAC's release.**
- Removed: **USB DAC bit-perfect routing.** Exclusive USB DAC is the bit-perfect path.

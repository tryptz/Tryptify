# Tryptify 1.8.9

## Changed

- **Shuffle is on the player**, in the dock's second slot next to Lyrics, and lights up while it's on. It used to be two taps into the top bar's overflow menu.
- **The sleep timer moved into Audio tools**, where it shows the minutes left rather than "Off". It takes the Output card's place — that one only ever said "Default" and opened Settings, which the headphone button does already.
- **Play, pause and the skip chevrons are redrawn**, built from exact geometry so the corners match each other and the two chevrons sit apart rather than nested.
- **The album-art glow has its own radius and brightness**, under the switch that draws it. Turning the lyric glow down no longer takes the cover's halo with it; both follow the lyric glow until you move them.

## Fixed

- **The artwork fills the player again on 3-button navigation.** The player was reserving the button bar's height twice, so the cover shrank and left gutters down both sides with a strip of flat background under the controls.
- **Glass no longer frosts into a grey slab on a light theme.** The player's panes and the mixer strips were washing against the theme's background instead of the dark backdrop they actually sit on.
- **The dock's press dome appears under the button you pressed**, instead of sliding across from the last one.
- **The band of light across the scrubber is gone.** The album glow was landing at mid-screen with its edge there; it sits behind the artwork now and fades out without a rim.
- **A lit dock button glows the shape of its icon**, not a soft square.

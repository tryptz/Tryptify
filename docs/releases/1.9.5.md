# Tryptify 1.9.5

**Tryptify Connect**: your phone and tryptify.com see each other, move the music between them at the same second, and follow each other's song. **Select on every song list**, with uploads of a whole selection to Tryptify Cloud. **My server's songs and search**, and **a password for your own TrypT HiFi server**. **Your StepMania packs as songs** in your library, **a frame rate limit** with an FPS counter, and a lighter CRT effect. On tryptify.com, in the app's eight languages, a web player with a **full-screen view of the lyrics and the app's waterfall**, coloured by the song's cover.

Each line is marked New, Changed, Removed or Fixed. Every detail is in the [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md).

- New: **Your devices, together.** Signed in, your phone and tryptify.com/player see each other. The Devices button in the player lists them and which one is playing.
- New: **Move the music** to any of them with a tap, at the same second, the queue coming too, or take it with Play here.
- New: **Follow the other device.** While your phone is quiet and another device plays, the mini player shows that song with its progress running in step, and its buttons steer it.
- New: **A seek bar for the other device**, in the Connect panel, with the elapsed and total time.
- New: **One plays at a time.** When another device starts playing, this one pauses.
- New: **Next to no battery.** Connected only while the app is on screen or playing.

## Library

- New: **Select on every song list**, from a song's ⋮ menu: the Local page, My server, local albums, artists and genres, and search results from your phone or your server. On a genre chart, hold a row.
- New: **A selection keeps its songs** on the Local page's long Songs list, however far you scroll.
- New: **Your StepMania packs as songs.** Settings › Library › Music folders › Add StepMania packs: every song of a pack linked from a folder plays like any other, with its chart's title, artist and picture, each pack an album.
- Fixed: **No doubles.** A pack song is listed once in Trypt Mania, with its chart, never counted as needing one. Trypt Mania's own pack folder no longer adds a second copy of every song you charted to your library.

## Your own music server

- New: **A Songs list** in Library › My server, beside Albums and Artists.
- New: **Search your server's songs** as you type.

## Tryptify Cloud

- New: **Upload a whole selection.** The selection bar's cloud button sends every picked song that's a file on your phone, with one message when they're queued and one when they're done.
- Changed: **Tryptify Cloud folds under its title** in Settings › Connections.

## Connections

- New: **A password for your server.** A TrypT HiFi server can ask for one; give it under the address in Settings › Connections › Add API. It stays on your phone and is never synced.
- Changed: **Plain http:// only to servers on your own network**, so a sign-in never crosses the internet unencrypted.

## Trypt Mania (Beta)

- Changed: **No chart server built in.** Charts, and lyrics timed by ear, use a server you add under Settings › Connections. Multiplayer's rooms server is still built in.
- New: **A song too big to share goes smaller.** In a room or a listening party, a song over the server's limit is turned into Opus at 320 kbps before it goes up.

## Theme Store

- Changed: **Cards show the lyrics and the player** from a theme's picture, so more fit a screen. A theme's own page still shows it whole.

## The app

- New: **A frame rate limit**, in Settings › System › Performance: Max, 30, 45, 60, 90 or 120 frames a second for the app, its animations, Trypt Mania and the visualizer. Lower saves battery and heat.
- New: **An FPS counter**, in the same place: the frames drawn each second, the display's refresh rate and the limit.
- Changed: **Logs and crash reports hide sign-ins.**
- Changed: **Android backups leave every sign-in out.**

## Look and feel

- Changed: **The CRT effect is lighter.** Its CRT look does about half the work for the same picture, so it no longer drops frames over the app or Trypt Mania.
- Fixed: **The lyrics' letter wave moves under the CRT effect**, its speed linked to the music or not.
- Fixed: **The visualizer keeps drawing** after you leave the player and come back.

## On tryptify.com

- New: **A web player** at [tryptify.com/player](https://tryptify.com/player): your files, your music server, Tryptify Cloud and Trypt HiFi, through the app's AutoEQ.
- New: **A full-screen player**, from the bar's expand button: the cover and its lyrics, timed to the second, or the app's spectrum waterfall.
- New: **Tryptify Connect in the browser**, following your phone's song on the bar, with a seek bar and controls.
- New: **Your Cloud plays in the browser**, at [tryptify.com/account](https://tryptify.com/account).
- New: **tryptify.com in the app's eight languages**, following your browser's the first time.
- New: **Listening parties on tryptify.com.** Invites are tryptify.com/party links, which open in the app where it's installed; anyone else joins in the browser.
- New: **The web player takes its colours from the song's cover**, as the app's Dynamic Colors do.
- Changed: **The web player's bar is glass like the site's menu**, its icons cut out of it as in the app's mini player, its volume a glass tube. Another device's song shows on the same glass.
- Fixed: **Seeking TrypT HiFi's TIDAL songs** in the web player.
- New: **Report a bug or help build Tryptify**, on the [Contact](https://tryptify.com/contact) page.

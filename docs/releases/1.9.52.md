# Tryptify 1.9.52

**A playlist's songs can be dragged into a new order**, and **M3U playlists import** as playlists of your own music and **export** back to a file. A **Quick Settings tile releases the exclusive USB DAC** to other apps in one tap, and **the player's ⋮ menu is now the song menu**.

Each line is marked New, Changed, Removed or Fixed. Every detail is in the [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Playlists

- New: **Reorder a playlist's songs.** Reorder, in a playlist's ⋮ menu, puts a handle on every song: drag one to its new place. Each drop is saved at once, and Done or Back leaves the mode.
- New: **Import M3U playlists.** Library › Playlists › New playlist › File reads `.m3u` and `.m3u8` files. Their songs are found in the music on your device, even if the list was written on a computer or for another format's copy; songs that aren't on the device are left out and counted.
- New: **Export a playlist as M3U.** Export as M3U, in a playlist's ⋮ menu, saves it as an `.m3u` file wherever you choose, for other players or another device. It lists the songs that are files on your device; songs that only stream are left out and counted.
- Fixed: **A song added after one was removed goes last**, instead of sharing the last song's place.

## Equalizer

- New: **System-wide AutoEQ from Quick Settings.** A new tile turns System-wide AutoEQ on and off over any app, and shows the headphones it corrects for. Add it in Settings › Equalizer, under System-wide AutoEQ.

## Player

- New: **Swipe the cover to see the next or previous song.** Its cover slides in beside the playing one as you swipe, and stays when you let go.
- Fixed: **The Ridgeline spectrum sits right on the bottom edge of the cover**, with no strip of cover under it.
- Fixed: **The player no longer stutters while a music link is on.** Glass is redrawn for a link only when that link moves the glass.
- Changed: **The player's menu is the song menu.** The player's ⋮ opens the same glass menu a song has in every list, so Like, Track info, Play in Trypt Mania, Play next and Add to queue are there too. Shuffle, repeat, Edit lyrics, Player Visuals Studio and Settings are at the end.
- Changed: **Menus and the player's sheets are frosted glass.** The song menu, New playlist and the player's Audio pipeline, Speed and Devices sheets now use the same glass as the audio tools sheet, so what is behind them is soft colour instead of text showing through.

## Playback

- New: **Atmos on headphones: a 15.1.12 virtual room.** On headphones, Atmos objects and beds now play from 27 virtual speakers and an LFE around you, in a room, with a limiter instead of a fixed cut in level. Settings › Audio › Atmos Renderer Configuration has how much room there is, and the switch.
- New: **Trapezoid panning.** Settings › Audio › Atmos Renderer: keeps high sounds overhead instead of out at the sides, by a shape you can edit (Ceiling width, Ceiling depth, Wall curve). Off by default.
- New: **See the sound in the room.** The 3D room is drawn by the GPU, with glowing speakers and the sound as fine grain in the air: dense round a speaker that plays, thin through the room for what it still holds.
- New: **The room follows you down the page.** Scroll past the 3D room and a picture of it stays at the top of the page; tap it to go back. A switch under the room turns the drawing of the sound off.
- New: **Shape your own room.** My room has presets (control room, club, concert hall and more, and an estimate of Stereo Montréal), rounded corners and leaning walls, all editable.
- New: **Choose the room your headphones play in.** Settings › Audio › Atmos Renderer › Room: the built-in room, a measured room from the room databases (Browse room database), or My room, set by its width, depth, height and damping. The 3D view takes the room's real shape and shows its reflections.
- New: **The Atmos room in 3D.** Settings › Audio › Atmos Renderer shows the 15.1.12 room your headphones play: every speaker lights up as it plays and the objects float where the mix puts them. Drag sideways to look round it.
- Fixed: **Binaural no longer cuts the top of the sound.** Everything above 15 kHz was lost in the headphone render; it reaches 20 kHz again.
- Fixed: **Binaural highs no longer sound hollow.** Voices and instruments in front had dips around 2 kHz and 5 to 10 kHz; they are filled in.
- Fixed: **Binaural Atmos has its bass back.** The headphone render was thin between 60 and 250 Hz, as if part of the mix was missing.
- Fixed: **Atmos objects are where the mix puts them.** On phones every object's position was lost, so a mix played from one side and nothing moved. Objects now sit and travel where they should.
- Fixed: **Atmos mixes are decoded as they were mixed.** Three in four of a stream's object updates were being missed, so objects moved late and bled into each other. Every update is read now, the surround channels are turned back into phase, and the speaker render is no longer 3 dB quiet.
- Changed: **Atmos shows its objects, not “6 ch”.** An Atmos stream is a 5.1 core with the objects inside it. The Audio pipeline sheet and the Atmos Renderer Configuration page now count the objects and say whether they are playing, and the room map shows each one where the mix puts it.
- Fixed: **Atmos objects are placed and moved the way Dolby's own 5.1 has them.** The speaker render and the 15.1.12 room now follow that render to within 0.1 dB, and moves land on time.
- Fixed: **Streams mixed with "room balance" and default trims match their own 5.1**: side objects go to the surrounds and the surrounds sit 3 dB lower, as the stream asks.
- Changed: **Atmos is on by itself with headphones.** Until you set the switch, the binaural render is on when headphones are connected and off on the phone's speaker.
- Fixed: **A solo in the mixer no longer silences the centre and surrounds of a 5.1 or Atmos song** with FX on.
- Fixed: **Atmos settings apply to the song that is playing.** Turning on the binaural render, the speaker render or the fold used to do nothing until the next song. It now takes effect after a short gap.
- Fixed: **The mixer's Atmos upmix no longer stops songs from starting** when the fold is off, and no longer re-processes an Atmos render.

## Search

- Fixed: **Discover on TIDAL no longer breaks TIDAL search for everyone on the server.** TIDAL allows a server's account a set number of searches a day, and one Discover page could use them all, after which search failed until midnight UTC. Discover now sends a quarter as many, searches go out a few at a time, and a search the server refuses is no longer retried at once.

## About

- New: **Join the Discord.** Settings › About has a link to the Discord server, under Ko-fi and Patreon.

## Sharing

- New: **A shared song's preview starts where you are.** Share link on the song that is playing sends the moment you are at, and the preview Discord plays starts there.
- Changed: **Previews are lossless or untouched, and can be scrubbed.** Qobuz, Deezer and TIDAL previews are lossless with a short fade in and out; YouTube and SoundCloud previews keep the song's own sound, not re-encoded.

## Exclusive USB DAC

- New: **Release the DAC from Quick Settings.** A new tile pauses the music and hands the DAC to other apps in one tap; another tap takes it back. Add it in Settings › Audio, under Exclusive USB DAC.

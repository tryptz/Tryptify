# Tryptify 1.9.52

**A playlist's songs can be dragged into a new order**, and **M3U playlists import** as playlists of your own music and **export** back to a file. A **Quick Settings tile releases the exclusive USB DAC** to other apps in one tap, and **the player's ⋮ menu is now the song menu**.

Each line is marked New, Changed, Removed or Fixed. Every detail is in the [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Playlists

- New: **Reorder a playlist's songs.** Reorder, in a playlist's ⋮ menu, puts a handle on every song: drag one to its new place. Each drop is saved at once, and Done or Back leaves the mode.
- New: **Import M3U playlists.** Library › Playlists › New playlist › File reads `.m3u` and `.m3u8` files. Their songs are found in the music on your device, even if the list was written on a computer or for another format's copy; songs that aren't on the device are left out and counted.
- New: **Export a playlist as M3U.** Export as M3U, in a playlist's ⋮ menu, saves it as an `.m3u` file wherever you choose, for other players or another device. It lists the songs that are files on your device; songs that only stream are left out and counted.
- Fixed: **A song added after one was removed goes last**, instead of sharing the last song's place.

## Player

- Changed: **The player's menu is the song menu.** The player's ⋮ opens the same glass menu a song has in every list, so Like, Track info, Play in Trypt Mania, Play next and Add to queue are there too. Shuffle, repeat, Edit lyrics, Player Visuals Studio and Settings are at the end.
- Changed: **Menus and the player's sheets are frosted glass.** The song menu, New playlist and the player's Audio pipeline, Speed and Devices sheets now use the same glass as the audio tools sheet, so what is behind them is soft colour instead of text showing through.

## Sharing

- New: **A shared song's preview starts where you are.** Share link on the song that is playing sends the moment you are at, and the preview Discord plays starts there.
- Changed: **Previews are lossless or untouched, and can be scrubbed.** Qobuz, Deezer and TIDAL previews are lossless with a short fade in and out; YouTube and SoundCloud previews keep the song's own sound, not re-encoded.

## Exclusive USB DAC

- New: **Release the DAC from Quick Settings.** A new tile pauses the music and hands the DAC to other apps in one tap; another tap takes it back. Add it in Settings › Audio, under Exclusive USB DAC.

# Tryptify 1.9.6

**Dolby Atmos on headphones plays from a 15.1.12 virtual room** that you can see in 3D and shape yourself. **System-wide AutoEQ corrects each ear, keeps running with the app closed, and has an exact mode through Shizuku.** The mixer gains a **Binaural Room**, which the **Crossfeed page can play on its own with its own HRTF and an LFE**, **the exclusive USB DAC plays surround** and has a **Quick Settings tile**, **the library reads your folders itself**, and **playlists can be reordered and moved as M3U files**.

Each line is marked New, Changed, Removed or Fixed. Every detail is in the [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Dolby Atmos

All of it is on Settings › Audio › Atmos Renderer Configuration.

- New: **A 15.1.12 virtual room on headphones.** Atmos objects and beds play from 27 virtual speakers and an LFE around you. Binaural render (SOFA HRTF) turns it on; Room amount sets how much of the room you hear.
- New: **The room in 3D**, at the top of the page: speakers light as they play and objects sit where the mix puts them. Drag sideways to turn it. Scrolled past, a small picture of it stays in the top right; tap it to go back.
- New: **Choose the room.** Under Room: Built-in room, a measured room from Browse room database (files up to 512 MB, with a progress line), or My room.
- New: **My room** is set by Width, Depth, Height, Damping and Roundness, or from ten presets. Save preset keeps yours; Export and Import pass one on as a file.
- New: **Trapezoid panning**, under Panning: keeps high sounds overhead instead of out at the sides, shaped by Ceiling width, Ceiling depth and Wall curve. Off by default.
- New: **Ear tuner**, under HRTF: drag the dot sideways for ear size and up or down for how strongly sounds above are marked.
- New: **Bigger HRTF sets.** A `.sofa` file can be up to 512 MB (it was 48 MB), and each virtual speaker plays the file's own measurement for its direction where it has one.
- New: **A ? in the top bar** opens "How the Atmos renderer works", ten short sections.
- Changed: **The built-in HRTF is the Neumann KU100** (SADIE II, University of York), measured every 5° by 10° up to straight overhead. The old set stopped 60° up.
- Changed: **Binaural render is on by itself** with wired, Bluetooth or 2-channel USB audio connected, and off on the phone's speaker, until you set the switch.
- Changed: **An Atmos stream shows its objects, not "6 ch".** The Audio pipeline sheet and the page count the objects and say whether they are being rendered.
- Removed: **Render Atmos to speakers.** The downmix settings and TIDAL Dolby Atmos are on Settings › Audio only.
- Fixed: **Atmos objects are where the mix puts them.** On phones every moving object's position was lost: they all played from one speaker and none moved.
- Fixed: **Every object update in a stream is read.** Three in four were missed on the test clip, so objects moved late.
- Fixed: **Binaural render and Downmix multichannel to stereo apply to the song that is playing**, after a short gap, instead of from the next song.

## Equalizer

- New: **Exact parametric EQ (Shizuku)** (Beta), under System-wide AutoEQ: other apps play through your real parametric filters instead of a 31-band copy of them. It needs Android 13 or newer and the Shizuku app running and allowing Tryptify; the setup steps are shown the first time, and a tap on the Shizuku line shows them again. Without Shizuku the standard system-wide EQ plays. Tried on one phone so far.
- Changed: **System-wide AutoEQ corrects each ear.** With 2-channel calibration on and a right-ear correction of its own, other apps get a curve an ear.
- Changed: **System-wide AutoEQ keeps running** with Tryptify closed or swiped away, under a notification. After the phone restarts it returns when Tryptify is opened. A limiter acts only when a boost would clip.
- New: **A Quick Settings tile for System-wide AutoEQ**, added from the Quick Settings tile row in Settings › Equalizer.
- New: **Offline measurements.** The download icon in the headphone list saves every measurement to the phone, so AutoEQ works with no connection. It can be stopped and carried on later.
- Changed: **The headphone search** ignores spaces and punctuation, finds words in any order, and forgives a typo when nothing matches exactly.
- Fixed: **An AutoEQ preset keeps its headphone.** Loading one puts its headphone and curves back in the picker and on the graph, with no network. A preset saved before this update clears the picker.
- Fixed: **Music through the exclusive USB DAC is corrected with System-wide AutoEQ on.** It had no correction at all.

## Mixer

- New: **Binaural Room**, under Space in FX Chain › Add Effect: your stereo plays from two speakers in the Atmos headphone room, through the built-in ear filters. My room gives it your own Width, Depth, Height and Damping. On the master of a surround mix it is one room, each channel at its own speaker.
- New: **Ten Binaural Room presets**, on the mixer's card and on the Crossfeed page's Room. Six real set-ups: Studio Monitors, Control Room, Living Room, Cinema, Club and Concert Hall, each ringing as long as such a room does (0.25 s for the control room, 2 s for the hall). Four that enlarge the space: Gentle Crossfeed, Wide Stage, Wrap Around and Deep Stage. All ten play at the same loudness.
- Changed: **The Atmos Upmix 9.1.6 preset** steers the song in eight bands. Before the preset's own bus effects, the upmix folds back to stereo as the song, sample for sample.
- Fixed: **Low bands sit where their knob says** in the 10-band and 3-band EQ, Filter, and the low cuts of Reverb, Delay, Misstortion and Disperser. 31 Hz played at 44 Hz on a CD-rate song and at 192 Hz on a 192 kHz one. A saved mix with such a band sounds different now.
- Fixed: **A solo no longer silences the centre, LFE and surrounds** of a 5.1 or Atmos song.
- Fixed: **Atmos Upmix 9.1.6 no longer stops songs from starting** with Downmix multichannel to stereo off; the upmix is then not applied.


## Crossfeed

Hold Crossfeed in the player's Audio tools to open its page.

- New: **Off, Crossfeed or Room.** One choice in place of the switch. Room is the mixer's Binaural Room on its own, with its Room amount, Binaural, Speaker angle, Output level and My room. An Atmos song played by the binaural render keeps that render's room. The player's Crossfeed switch turns on the one last chosen.
- New: **An HRTF for the Room**, inside it: the built-in Neumann KU100 or any .sofa on the phone, from Browse HRTF database or Load .sofa file. It does not change the Atmos page's.
- New: **LFE: bass outside the room.** The bass below a crossover from 40 to 200 Hz plays straight to both ears as one channel, at its own level. The split is linear phase and adds about 21 ms of delay.
- New: **2D or 3D**, for Crossfeed and Room alike.
- Fixed: **Crossfeed is kept when the app restarts.** It was off again at every start.
## Player

- New: **The player follows your other device.** While this phone plays nothing and another of your devices plays (Tryptify Connect), the player shows that song: its cover, lyrics and time, and its controls steer that device. Before, only the mini player did.
- New: **Visualizer under the lyrics**, in Player Visuals Studio › Lyrics and again with the 3D spectrogram's settings: the Lyrics view keeps the cover's Spectrum, Wave Candy or 3D spectrogram along the bottom, with the lyrics on top. On the 3D spectrogram the words being sung are a light: the water mirrors their glow and the peaks throw their shadows away from them (Light from the lyrics sets how much). The cover's choice of view is also kept now across a trip to the lyrics and back.
- New: **A 3D spectrogram on the cover.** The cover's visualizer button walks Spectrum, Wave Candy and now the 3D spectrogram: your music as a sheet of water, time sideways, bass at the front, in the album's colour with its loudest tops in the album's second colour. From Android 13 it is lit pixel by pixel: ripples, a low sun whose shadows the peaks throw across the water, and light glowing through the swells (Android 10 and later for a simpler version). Settings › 3D spectrogram sets the speed (down to half a second across), the FFT's size and overlap (a spectrum for every hop of sound, so a fast speed shows detail and not steps), the range in dB, attack and release, camera angle, peak height, gloss, subsurface glow, water ripples, shadows, smoothness, depth haze, pitch range and quality, with a preview you steer by touch. **Behind the player** plays it as the player's background instead: along the bottom of the screen, edge to edge, under the title, the controls and the lyrics.
- New: **The other device's volume**, a slider in the Tryptify Connect panel. On tryptify.com/player the volume slider does the same for the phone. The phone's volume keys stay the phone's own.
- New: **Line start and Soft focus.** Player Visuals Studio › Lyrics › Typography: From the left starts every line at the left, and Soft focus blurs and dims the lines away from the sung one (Android 12 and later).
- New: **Light transition for the god rays**: a switch, and a Transition time from 0 to 1000 ms.
- New: **When a word ends**, for the god rays: Fade out, or Keep shining, which keeps the light on a word until the next one starts (following the whole line, on the line until the next line starts). Light transition is now first in the God rays section.
- New: **Previews sing whole lyrics.** Player Visuals Studio and a theme's page in the Theme Store play made-up lyrics through the real lyric view, word by word, so the highlight, god rays and transitions show as in the player. A playing song's own lyrics still come first in the Studio.
- Changed: **Through a song's intro the first lyric line waits where the sung line will be**, clear, with the lines below it dimmed. It sat in the middle of the screen with every line equally bright.
- New: **An "Apple Music" lyric preset.**
- Changed: **The player's ⋮ opens the song menu**, so Play next, Add to queue, Play in Trypt Mania, Like and Track info are there too. Shuffle, Repeat, Edit lyrics, Player Visuals Studio and Settings follow at the end.
- Changed: **Swiping the cover** slides the next or previous song's cover in beside the playing one.
- Changed: **The song menu and the player's Audio pipeline, Speed and Tryptify Connect sheets are frosted glass.**
- Fixed: **God rays stay off until something is sung**, a song's intro included; with word-timed lyrics they go out when the word ends.
- Fixed: **Lyrics with god rays draw faster**: the shafts are drawn at half size and scaled up. With the rays on, the lyric view had run at 62 to 72 frames a second against 119 without, on a 165 Hz phone.
- Fixed: **Lyric letters are no longer cut off at the side.** Edge margin moves the text only.
- Fixed: **The Ridgeline spectrum's ground reaches the bottom edge of the cover**, with no strip of cover under it.

## Library

- Changed: **The library reads your folders itself.** With folders picked, a new file shows up without waiting for Android to index it.
- Changed: **A file that moves stays the song it was**, with its likes, playlists, history, edits and found cover. Removing the old folder and adding the new one works in either order, and songs on a memory card that is out are kept.
- Changed: **A new tag reader** (TagLib). Old tags not in UTF-8 are read by your phone's languages. The library is read again once after the update.
- Fixed: **WMA tags are read.**
- Fixed: **A liked or playlisted file of your own is no longer taken for a TIDAL song.** Go to album on one said "HTTP 404", and its source read TIDAL; it reads Local now. A TIDAL song queued from Search had the same 404.
- Fixed: **TIDAL downloads show their cover** on the Downloads screen.
- Fixed: **Music-server songs played from their copy on the phone show their cover** in the notification, on the lock screen and over Bluetooth, Tryptify Cloud included.

## Playlists

- New: **Reorder**, in the ⋮ menu of a playlist of your own, puts a handle on every song: drag one to its new place. Each drop is saved at once.
- New: **Import M3U.** Library › Playlists › Create playlist › File › M3U reads `.m3u` and `.m3u8` files. Their songs are looked up in the music on your device; those not found are left out and counted.
- New: **Export as M3U**, in a playlist's ⋮ menu, writes the songs that are files on your device. Songs that only stream, and downloads kept inside the app, are left out and counted.
- Fixed: **A song added after one was removed goes last.**

## Exclusive USB DAC

- New: **Surround.** A 5.1 or 7.1 mix stays surround on a multichannel USB interface or AVR in exclusive mode; a narrower mix plays in the first channels, untouched. Settings › Audio shows the device's Channels. Not yet tried on a multichannel device.
- New: **A Quick Settings tile** for the switch: a tap pauses the music and turns exclusive mode off, another turns it back on. Add it from the Quick Settings tile row under the switch.
- Changed: **The DAC is not taken while Android is playing to it.** A song starts on the DAC only once Android has had 4 seconds of quiet. If that has not come after 8 seconds while playing, the song goes through Android until the next pause.

## Sharing

- New: **Liked and Saved in the Theme Store**, beside Popular, New and Mine: the themes you liked, and the ones you saved with the bookmark on a theme's page. Both follow your account.
- New: **Share link works for a file on your phone.** It is found in the catalogue by its ISRC, or by its name and a length within 2 seconds, and shared as that song. A catalogue song whose album is not known on the phone is found the same way.
- New: **A shared preview starts where you are**, when the song shared is the one playing or paused.
- Changed: **Sharing an album waits for its preview**, up to 50 seconds a server, saying "Getting … ready to share…". A link pasted before the preview was ready showed nothing in Discord.

## The app

- New: **Rendering quality.** Settings › System › Performance: Balanced and Fast draw the glass and the god rays smaller and scale them up, for more frames a second and less heat. High, the default, is as before.
- New: **Send logs**, the last row of Settings › System, sends the app's log once to Tryptify's developer, after a dialog that says what leaves the phone. It needs a sign-in; nothing is sent by itself.
- New: **Live log**, a chip on Settings › System › View debug log, sends the log to your own account as it is written, for someone helping you. It needs a sign-in, is off at every start and stops after 30 minutes.
- Changed: **The FPS counter notes slow frames** in the debug log while it is on screen.
- New: **Join the Discord**, on Settings › About, where the version line now shows the build's commit.
- Fixed: **Discover no longer uses up TIDAL's searches.** One Discover page could use all the searches TIDAL allows a server in a day, and search then failed for everyone on it. Discover asks for far fewer, and at most four go out at once.

# Tryptify 1.9.51

**Continuous playback**: when a playlist, album or folder ends, the next one on the screen you opened it from plays, and after the last, radio from its last song. Previous on a list's first song goes back to the list before, and **your playlists can be sorted**. **ReplayGain is back**, aimed at the loudness you pick in LUFS, without ever clipping. **Your own music server** counts your plays, stars what you like, lists its playlists and liked songs, sends its own lyrics and can stream smaller on mobile data. **Your files' own lyrics** come first, and USB DACs with one clock stay exclusive for CD-quality songs. In **Trypt Mania, chart your own songs** on Tryptify's server, your first one free, and **the game plays through your USB DAC and the DSP engine**. **Music links presets** save and share a whole set of links, and the app is **lighter on battery and memory**.

Each line is marked New, Changed, Removed or Fixed. Every detail is in the [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Continuous playback

- New: **Continue into the next list.** When a playlist, album or phone folder ends, the next one on the screen you opened it from plays: the next album on an artist's page (albums, then EPs and singles), in your liked albums, a search or a Discover shelf; the next playlist in your Library or on your music server; the next phone folder with songs, the whole tree top to bottom. After the last, radio from its last song. On by default, in Settings › Audio › Playback.
- New: **Previous goes back a list.** On a list's first song, Previous plays the last song of the list before.
- New: **The queue keeps every list played**, so the queue sheet scrolls back through them, and the count in the player runs from the first song.
- New: **It plays along with how you listen.** Repeat All still loops the list playing and Repeat One the song; under shuffle the next list plays shuffled; songs you add with Play next or Add to queue play first. It carries on after the app restarts.
- New: **Sort your playlists**, with the sort button beside New playlist in Library › Playlists: last edited, name or date created. Playback walks your playlists in that order.

## Playback

- New: **ReplayGain is back.** Settings › Audio › Audio processing › ReplayGain evens out loudness between songs from the ReplayGain (or R128) tags in your files, with one switch: an album played in order keeps its own balance, any other song is evened out on its own. Off by default.
- New: **Songs without tags are measured as they play**, streams included: within a second, a song louder than your target glides down to it. They are only ever turned down.
- New: **See what it does.** Under the switch, "This song" shows the gain on the song playing, and for a measured song the loudness it measured.
- New: **Pick a target loudness, in LUFS**: -23, -18 (ReplayGain's own), -16, -14 (what most streaming services play at, and the default) or -8. Turning ReplayGain on or off, or picking another target, changes the song playing at once.
- New: **A ReplayGain switch in the player's audio panel**, under Crossfeed. Hold it for Settings › Audio's other modes and the target.
- New: **It never clips.** Loud songs are turned down; a quiet song is turned up only as far as its file's peak tag leaves room. Your library reads its peak tags once, in the background, after the update. It works on every output, the USB DAC included, and at 0 dB bit-perfect stays bit-perfect.
- Fixed: **USB DACs with one clock keep exclusive mode for CD-quality songs.** A DAC that runs only 48 and 96 kHz, like the Nothing Ear (1) and Headphone (1), now plays 44.1 kHz songs resampled to its nearest rate instead of falling back to Android's mixer. Rates the DAC takes stay bit-perfect.

## Trypt Mania

- New: **Chart your own songs.** Generate charts on the menu charts a song on Tryptify's server: your first song is free, then minutes from a pack bought once through Creem, never a subscription: 150 minutes for $2.99, 500 for $9.99 or 1,000 for $19.99. A song counts its length rounded up to the minute, at least 2. A song that fails gives its minutes back, and charting on a server of your own, under Settings › Connections, stays free.
- New: **See what is charting**, with its progress and Cancel all, and **tap a song in the history** to open it in the song list.
- New: **Trypt Mania plays through your USB DAC.** With a DAC in exclusive mode, a run's song and the previews play through it, at the level your volume keys set. Starting a run pauses your music; press play in the player to take the DAC back.
- New: **The game plays through the DSP engine**: your AutoEQ, the mixer, the Oxford Inflator, Compressor and Crossfeed and ReplayGain, on the DAC and on the phone alike.
- New: **Settings › Audio in the game** switches AutoEQ, the Oxford Inflator and ReplayGain, the same settings the player uses.
- New: **Motion blur.** Notes can leave a short trail and the lyrics blur as they glide: Trypt Mania › Graphics › Motion blur and Player Visuals Studio › 3D letter wave. Off by default.
- Changed: **Hold + or − to nudge the audio offset**, in the Sync tab and in Settings › Audio › Bluetooth sync, instead of a tap per 5 ms.
- Changed: **The Groove Radar's labels** are in the card's own font and sit clear of the web.

## Music links

- New: **Presets.** Save every link on the Music links page under a name, load a set back in one tap with Undo, and export or import one as a file. The mixer's links stay with mixer presets.
- Fixed: **"Resume the link" reads in full** instead of "Resume the".

## Your own music server

- New: **Your server counts your plays**: now playing as a song starts, and the play once you've heard enough of it, so its play counts, Recently played and any Last.fm or ListenBrainz it passes plays on agree.
- New: **Liking a song stars it on your server**, and unliking unstars it, so your server's other apps agree.
- New: **Playlists and Liked songs in My server**: your server's playlists, to open, play or shuffle, and everything starred on it.
- New: **Your server's lyrics come first** for its songs: timed when it has them timed (Navidrome and other OpenSubsonic servers), plain otherwise.
- New: **Cellular streaming**, in Settings › Connections › Music server: keep the original file, or ask your server for an MP3 of at most 320, 192 or 128 kbps on mobile data. Wi-Fi always gets the original.

## Lyrics

- New: **A song's own lyrics come first**: the `.lrc` next to the file and lyrics stored in its tags, for library files and downloads. Word-timed ones are used straight away, and offline they're what shows.
- Fixed: **LRC lyrics that read wrong now read right**, from LRCLib too: a file starting with a byte order mark keeps its first line, an end stamp is a time rather than part of the lyric, a file stamped all `[00:00.00]` shows as plain lyrics, and `[mm:ss]` stamps, two stamps on a line, `[offset:]` and word timing read as they do everywhere else.
- Removed: **Lyrics timed on a chart server.** Lyrics timed before, and the ones you time in the lyrics editor, stay.

## Lighter and smoother

- Changed: **Less CPU while music plays**, screen off included, and **less memory**: one tapped song no longer keeps the whole library in memory, the queue keeps at most 2,000 played songs, and covers decode at the size shown.
- Changed: **A USB DAC wakes the phone far less**, about 375 times a second instead of 2,000, and a stream paused for ten seconds rests until you play.
- Changed: **Atmos takes about a third of the CPU** for 7.1.4 to headphones, **hi-res buffering is capped at 16 MB**, and glass screens open without recompiling their shader.
- Fixed: **ReplayGain no longer dips** at a crossfade or a seek, and keeps its loudness after the update for anyone who had it on.
- Fixed: **Continue into the next list keeps going** after a restart and on a weak connection, and **shuffle no longer replays the lists already played.**
- Fixed: **Bit-perfect stays bit-perfect after a fader move**, a loaded mixer preset is saved at once, and a library rescan keeps albums and artists as they were.
- Fixed: **Long sessions with the limiter on can no longer crash**, and plugging another USB device in while a DAC streams is safe.

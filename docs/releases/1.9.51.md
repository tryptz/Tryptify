# Tryptify 1.9.51

**YouTube and SoundCloud are built in**: search, play and download their songs, with YouTube running on your phone and no sign-in. **Share link** sends a tryptify.com link that plays in anyone's Tryptify, with a play button in Discord. When a list ends **the next one plays**, **ReplayGain is back** and **Trypt Mania charts your own songs**, with auto play and a race against your best. Your **music server** does more, a local song's **info can be edited**, and the home screen gets a **glass widget**.

Each line is marked New, Changed, Removed or Fixed. Every detail is in the [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## YouTube and SoundCloud

- New: **Two more sources.** Search YouTube and SoundCloud with their own chips, then play or download their songs. YouTube search finds plain uploads too, not only YouTube Music's songs.
- New: **YouTube runs on your phone itself**, with no sign-in. SoundCloud goes through Tryptify's server, with your Tryptify sign-in.
- New: **Locked songs are left out** of search: SoundCloud's DRM-locked label uploads, which say so if played.
- New: **Both are lossy**, as they stream; lossless stays with TIDAL, Qobuz and Deezer.

## Sharing

- New: **Share link**, in a song's or the player's menu and on an album's page: a tryptify.com link that plays in anyone's Tryptify, on any service they have. Links shared from TrypT-hifi play here too.
- New: **YouTube and SoundCloud songs too.** Signed in, Discord shows their cover with a play button for a 30-second preview.
- New: **YouTube fills the gaps.** A SoundCloud link opened signed out, or a shared song none of your services has, plays from YouTube.

## Continuous playback

- New: **Continue into the next list.** When a playlist, album or folder ends, the next one on the screen you opened it from plays, then radio (Settings › Audio › Playback).
- New: **Previous goes back a list**, and **your playlists can be sorted** in Library › Playlists, the order playback follows.

## Playback

- New: **ReplayGain is back**, in Settings › Audio › Audio processing and the player's audio panel. It evens songs out to a loudness you pick (-23 to -8 LUFS), measures untagged songs as they play, and never clips.
- New: **Deezer in hi-res**: 24-bit FLAC up to 192 kHz, from your TrypT HiFi server. Deezer's quality picker is now Deezer's own three: Hi-Res FLAC, MP3 320 kbps and MP3 128 kbps.
- Changed: **The player's title uses the whole width**, out to the heart, with the artist and the heart on the line under it.
- Changed: **The tempo is measured over the whole song** for a song on your phone with no BPM tag, in the Speed panel's BPM and for generated charts.
- Fixed: **USB DACs with one clock stay exclusive** for CD-quality songs.
- Fixed: **The Oxford Compressor no longer distorts bass** with a fast release.

## Your own music server

- New: **It counts your plays, stars what you like and sends its own lyrics.** My server lists its playlists and liked songs.
- New: **Cellular streaming**: a smaller MP3 on mobile data, in Settings › Connections › Music server.

## Library

- New: **Edit info** in a local song's ⋮ menu fixes its title, artist, album and more. Tryptify keeps the edit and leaves the file as it is; Last.fm gets it from the song's next play.
- Fixed: **Local songs are scrobbled under their own artist**, not the album artist of a label's compilation.

## Lyrics

- New: **A song's own lyrics come first**: the `.lrc` beside the file and lyrics in its tags, offline too.
- Fixed: **LRC files read right**, LRCLib's included, and **the lyrics pump to the kick drum**, not the vocals.
- Fixed: **The curve over the sung line or word no longer tears or leans** (Player Visuals Studio › 3D Letter Wave › Curve).
- Removed: **Lyrics timed on a chart server.** Lyrics already timed stay.

## Trypt Mania

- New: **Chart your own songs** on Tryptify's server, with progress and a history. Your first song is free, then minute packs bought once, from 150 minutes for $2.99; your own server stays free.
- New: **The game plays through your USB DAC and the DSP engine**, with AutoEQ and ReplayGain in its own Settings › Audio.
- New: **Motion blur** for the notes and the gliding lyrics, off by default.
- New: **Themes from StepMania.** Themes, on Trypt Mania's menu, imports StepMania noteskins, themes and announcers from a ZIP, .smzip, 7z or RAR file. A theme changes a run's judgements, combo, fonts, background and hit sounds; the menus stay as they are.
- New: **Browse the StepMania Archive** from Themes: tap a download and it imports in the background.
- New: **Characters dance behind the field**, on the song's beat. Themes › Sizes sets how big the dancer, the judgement and the combo are drawn.
- New: **Auto play** plays a run for you, never saved, and **Race your best** puts your best run beside you as a score (Player options › Judge).
- Changed: **Generated charts follow the song**: sparser verses, fuller choruses, bursts on Hard and Challenge, and patterns that change. Generate a chart again to get the new one.
- Changed: **A run ends with its song**: it plays to the end (tap to skip), then stripes fade to black before the results.
- Changed: **Hold + or − to nudge the audio offset.**
- Changed: **Bigger judgements that land with a pop**, hardest for a Marvelous. With reduced motion they stay still.
- Changed: **The song preview plays on through the menus**, until a run, the editor or leaving Trypt Mania.
- Fixed: **The Player options preview runs at your real speed**, with the notes as far apart as in the run.
- Fixed: **Generated charts keep the beat**: no more drift on songs between whole tempos, and no grid starting a beat late.
- Fixed: **Tap to sync counts every tap** from the first, and no longer reads a whole click off.
- Fixed: **Pack music and pictures stay.** Your pack and imported packs keep to separate folders, and pack pictures no longer vanish when space runs low.

## Music links

- New: **Presets.** Save every link under a name, load a set in one tap with Undo, and share one as a file.
- Changed: **Each link hears its own band**, so a kick link no longer falls when the vocals rise, links move again right after a pause or a new song, and loud, heavily mastered songs move them too.
- Fixed: **"Resume the link" reads in full.**

## Home screen widget

- New: **A glass widget** with the cover and a play button, in three resizable sizes. Settings › Appearance › Home screen widget sets its look, with a live preview.

## Lighter and smoother

- Changed: **Less CPU and memory while music plays**, and USB DACs wake the phone far less.
- Fixed: **ReplayGain no longer dips** at a crossfade or seek, **continuous playback keeps going** after a restart and under shuffle, **bit-perfect survives a fader move**, and two rare crashes are gone.

## Debug log

- Fixed: **Copy no longer crashes the app** on a large log. It copies the newest lines and says when the rest needs Export to file.

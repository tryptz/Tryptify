# Tryptify 1.9.51

**Continuous playback**: when a playlist, album or folder ends, the next one on the screen you opened it from plays, and after the last, radio from its last song. Previous on a list's first song goes back to the list before, and **your playlists can be sorted**. **ReplayGain is back**, aimed at the loudness you pick in LUFS, without ever clipping. **Your own music server** counts your plays, stars what you like, lists its playlists and liked songs, sends its own lyrics and can stream smaller on mobile data. **Your files' own lyrics** come first, and USB DACs with one clock stay exclusive for CD-quality songs.

Each line is marked New, Changed, Removed or Fixed. Every detail is in the [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Continuous playback

- New: **Continue into the next list.** When a playlist, album or phone folder ends, the next one on the screen you opened it from plays: the next album on an artist's page (albums, then EPs and singles), in your liked albums, a search or a Discover shelf; the next playlist in your Library or on your music server; the next phone folder with songs, the whole tree top to bottom. After the last, radio from its last song. On by default, in Settings › Audio › Playback.
- New: **Previous goes back a list.** On a list's first song, Previous plays the last song of the list before.
- New: **The queue keeps every list played**, so the queue sheet scrolls back through them, and the count in the player runs from the first song.
- New: **It plays along with how you listen.** Repeat All still loops the list playing and Repeat One the song; under shuffle the next list plays shuffled; songs you add with Play next or Add to queue play first. It carries on after the app restarts.
- New: **Sort your playlists**, with the sort button beside New playlist in Library › Playlists: last edited, name or date created. Playback walks your playlists in that order.

## Playback

- New: **ReplayGain is back.** Settings › Audio › Audio processing › ReplayGain evens out loudness between songs from the ReplayGain (or R128) tags in your files: Track, Album, or Smart, which uses the album's level while an album plays in order. Off by default.
- New: **Pick a target loudness, in LUFS**: -23, -18 (ReplayGain's own), -16, -14 (what most streaming services play at, and the default) or -8. Turning ReplayGain on or off, or picking another target, changes the song playing at once.
- New: **It never clips.** Loud songs are turned down; a song is turned up only when its file shows there's room. It works on every output, the USB DAC included, and at 0 dB bit-perfect stays bit-perfect.
- Fixed: **USB DACs with one clock keep exclusive mode for CD-quality songs.** A DAC that runs only 48 and 96 kHz, like the Nothing Ear (1) and Headphone (1), now plays 44.1 kHz songs resampled to its nearest rate instead of falling back to Android's mixer. Rates the DAC takes stay bit-perfect.

## Your own music server

- New: **Your server counts your plays**: now playing as a song starts, and the play once you've heard enough of it, so its play counts, Recently played and any Last.fm or ListenBrainz it passes plays on agree.
- New: **Liking a song stars it on your server**, and unliking unstars it, so your server's other apps agree.
- New: **Playlists and Liked songs in My server**: your server's playlists, to open, play or shuffle, and everything starred on it.
- New: **Your server's lyrics come first** for its songs: timed when it has them timed (Navidrome and other OpenSubsonic servers), plain otherwise.
- New: **Cellular streaming**, in Settings › Connections › Music server: keep the original file, or ask your server for an MP3 of at most 320, 192 or 128 kbps on mobile data. Wi-Fi always gets the original.

## Lyrics

- New: **A song's own lyrics come first**: the `.lrc` next to the file and lyrics stored in its tags, for library files and downloads. Word-timed ones are used straight away, and offline they're what shows.
- Fixed: **LRC lyrics that read wrong now read right**, from LRCLib too: a file starting with a byte order mark keeps its first line, an end stamp is a time rather than part of the lyric, a file stamped all `[00:00.00]` shows as plain lyrics, and `[mm:ss]` stamps, two stamps on a line, `[offset:]` and word timing read as they do everywhere else.

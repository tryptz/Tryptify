# Tryptify 1.9.2

A new way around the app, a spectrum that shows the last few seconds of the song, real loudness metering and Deezer. The player also does less work, so it uses less battery.

## Highlights

### Glass tab bar
Home, Discover, Radio, Library and Search now sit in one glass bar at the bottom of every page. Scroll down and the mini player folds into it; scroll up and it comes back. Home shows Recently Played and Liked Songs, Library switches sections with chips, and Search is its own page.

### Spectrum waterfall
The spectrum on the album art now falls back in time. The bright front line is what you hear now; older lines recede, rise and fade behind it like a mountain range.

- Four styles: Lines, Ridgeline, Heat and Neon.
- Adjust depth, fade start, angle and line weight in Player Visuals Studio.
- Touch the preview to tune it: drag up and down for the angle, left and right for the depth, pinch for line weight, or drag the fade line.
- Cap the frame rate from 15 to 120 fps or leave it on Max. Vsync keeps frames evenly spaced; turn it off for the exact rate.
- The preview runs on a demo signal when nothing is playing.

### LUFS meters
Broadcast standard loudness (EBU R128) for what Tryptify sends to your output.

- The Audio Pipeline panel has a new Loudness stage: Momentary, Short-term, Integrated, Loudness Range and True Peak.
- The mixer's master strip shows Short-term and Integrated. Tap it to restart Integrated.
- The meter only runs while a readout is on screen and restarts with each track.

### Deezer
Search Deezer's songs, albums and artists, then play or download them in full as FLAC or MP3 320.

## Player and visuals
- Wave Candy: an FL Studio style oscilloscope on the cover, stereo or mono, with a neon or shadow glow.
- Kick punch: the cover jumps on every kick drum.
- Default Preset in Settings opens the glass preset browser, with categories, authors, favourites and search across all 9,795 presets.
- A lighter player: the spectrum no longer redraws the whole player, it rests while paused, and only one settings preview runs at a time.
- The Search button swells under your finger like the other tabs.

## Search and sources
- Every song, album and artist shows the catalog it came from.
- Add an API server once and Tryptify detects which catalogs it serves.
- Source filters cover every result type, and albums open from their own catalog.
- When TIDAL can't play a song, you choose whether Qobuz plays it.

## Mixer and audio
- Route any bus to any bus, up to 48, with cables and send knobs.
- Atmos Upmix 9.1.6 spreads stereo into 9.1.6 surround.
- Mastering style presets put the master at 0 dB with a limiter last.
- Crossfades now work on a USB DAC and no longer drop out.

## Speed
- Tempo is detected for each song, and speed can be set in BPM, multiplier or semitones.
- Push the bar under the BPM to bend the tempo like a turntable, or long-press the BPM to type one.

## Library
- Float and 8-bit WAVs play correctly, show their tags and covers, and fill in the artist from "Artist ~ Title" names.
- A fast double back no longer blanks the screen.

## Removed
- The Nightcore button. Use 1.10x with Preserve pitch off instead.
- The catalog picker and URL fields, replaced by the API list.

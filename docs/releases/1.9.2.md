# Tryptify 1.9.2

This release is mostly about finding your way and seeing what you hear: a glass tab bar on every page, a spectrum that shows the last few seconds of the song, and loudness meters for what leaves the player. Deezer joins search, and the player does less work while it plays. Tryptify now speaks seven languages, the visualizer no longer crashes, and the download is 31 MB smaller.

## New

- **Seven languages.** English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, French, Spanish, Turkish and German. Choose one in Settings › Appearance › Language, or in Android's own app languages list on Android 13 and later. Tab names shrink to fit rather than being cut off.
- **A glass tab bar on every page.** Home, Discover, Radio, Library and Search sit in one bar at the bottom, tinted by whatever is playing. Scroll down and the mini player folds into it; scroll up and it comes back.
- **Tabs or mini player, not both.** Turn on Hide mini player when the tabs show, in Settings › Library › Tab bar, and the bar switches between the tabs and the mini player as you scroll. Tap the tab you're on to bring the player back on a page too short to scroll.
- **New Home, Library and Search.** Home shows Recently Played and Liked Songs, Library switches sections with chips, and Search is a page of its own.
- **The spectrum falls back in time.** The bright front line on the album art is what you hear now, and older lines recede, rise and fade behind it like a mountain range. Choose Lines, Ridgeline, Heat or Neon, and set the depth, fade, angle and line weight in Player Visuals Studio.
- **Glass and Legacy spectrum styles.** Glass draws the spectrum as one line of liquid glass that bends the cover beneath it. Legacy brings back the filled spectrum from before the waterfall.
- **Analyse the spectrum like a studio meter.** Choose Live average, Live max, Average or Max, set the averaging time and the overlap with knobs, and draw the line in the album's colour or one you pick.
- **Tune the spectrum with your finger.** Drag the Studio's preview up and down for the angle and sideways for the depth, pinch for line weight, or drag the fade line itself. It runs on a demo signal when nothing is playing.
- **A frame rate cap for the spectrum.** Draw it at anything from 15 to 120 fps, or every refresh. Vsync keeps the frames evenly spaced; with it off you get the exact rate.
- **Loudness meters.** The Audio Pipeline panel has a Loudness stage with Momentary, Short-term, Integrated, Loudness Range and True Peak, to the broadcast standard (EBU R128). The mixer's master strip shows Short-term and Integrated; tap it to start Integrated over.
- **Deezer in search.** Find Deezer's songs, albums and artists, and play or download them in full as FLAC or MP3 320.
- **Every result says where it is from.** Songs, albums and artists show their catalog, and the player says "via" when a track plays from a different one.
- **One list of APIs.** Add a server once and Tryptify works out which catalogs it serves. It replaces the catalog picker and the URL fields.
- **Wave Candy and the kick punch.** An FL Studio style oscilloscope runs along the bottom of the cover, and the cover jumps on every kick drum.
- **Route any bus to any bus.** Up to 48 buses, with cables and send knobs, and a preset that spreads stereo into 9.1.6 surround.
- **Tempo in BPM.** Each song's tempo is detected and speed can be set in BPM. Push the bar under it to bend the tempo like a turntable, or long-press to type one.
- **Crossfades on a USB DAC.** Crossfade now works while Tryptify drives your DAC directly, across sample rates.

## Changed

- **A 31 MB smaller download.** The visualizer's 9,795 presets were packed into the app twice.
- **Visual Studio opens in one tap.** Its chip in Settings goes straight to the Studio.
- **Default Preset opens the preset browser.** The setting used to be one menu of all 9,795 presets. It now opens the same glass browser as the player, with categories, authors, favourites and search.
- **The player does less work.** The spectrum no longer redraws the whole player many times a second, it stops when playback pauses, and only one settings preview runs at a time. The glass no longer keeps the motion sensor on when nothing uses it.
- **TIDAL asks before falling back.** When TIDAL can't play a song, you choose whether Qobuz plays it, instead of the player switching quietly.
- **Mastering style mixer presets.** The master sits at 0 dB with a limiter last. Tap a strip to select it, and again to open it.
- **The Search button presses like the other tabs.** Its glass swells under your finger instead of dimpling.
- **The Nightcore button is gone.** Use 1.10x with Preserve pitch off for the same sound.

## Fixed

- **The visualizer no longer crashes the app.** Presets that use pictures Tryptify doesn't ship crashed it on their first frame. All 9,795 now load. If one ever crashes your phone, it is flagged and skipped from then on, and you can clear the flags in the Studio's visualizer settings.
- **The waterfall spectrum no longer slows the player down.** It is drawn in one pass instead of thousands of small shapes a frame.
- **Crossfades no longer drop out.** The start of a fade could cut the sound for a moment, and speed and pitch now carry through it.
- **WAV files play and show properly.** Float and 8-bit WAVs play correctly, their tags and covers show, and files named "Artist ~ Title" fill in the artist.
- **A fast double back no longer blanks the screen.** Local songs also open their own artist and album.

Contact

For help with Tryptify Cloud, billing, refunds, your data, or anything else:

support@tryptify.com

We answer within 3 business days. For billing questions, include the email you used at checkout.

Report a bug or help build Tryptify

Found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead, and add your phone, its Android version and the app version (Settings › About).

Want to help build it? Tryptify is open source. Write to support@tryptify.com with what you would like to work on, such as code, translations, themes or testing, or pick up an open issue on GitHub.

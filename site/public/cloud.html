Tryptify Cloud

Your personal Subsonic-API music storage, made for fast streaming.

Upload your own music once and play it anywhere in Tryptify: no computer left on at home, no server to set up, no port forwarding.

See plans Get the app

How it works

  1. Sign in to Tryptify and open Settings › Connections › Tryptify Cloud.
  2. Pick a plan or start the free trial. Payment opens in your browser.
  3. Upload songs from the app: open a song's menu under Library › Local and tap Upload to Tryptify Cloud.
  4. Play them anywhere under Library › My server and in Search, at their original quality.

What you get

Built on the Subsonic API

Your library answers the open Subsonic API, the same one Navidrome speaks, so it browses and seeks like a home server.

Private to you

Only your account can see or play your library. Each device signs in with its own random password, never your account password.

Formats

FLAC, ALAC, MP3, AAC/M4A, Ogg Vorbis, Opus, WAV, AIFF, APE and WMA, up to 2 GB per file, with cover art.
